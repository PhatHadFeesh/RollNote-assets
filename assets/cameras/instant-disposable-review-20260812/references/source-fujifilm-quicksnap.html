Product Specifications

  • Film photography.
    Made easy.

    Start shooting on 135 film right away thanks to the built-in battery!
    No charging required!
    And to take a photo? Simply turn the dial and press the shutter button—it's that easy!

  • Light and compact!

    Our QuickSnap™ single-use film camera comes in at only 90 grams, making it half the weight of your average smartphone. And being super compact, it's easy to pop in your pocket and take with you, wherever you go!

  • 27 shots.
    27 moments.

    Each photo taken moves the counter down until you reach '0'. That means 27 beautifully candid photos captured on film ready to be sent for development!

  • Light up your moments with flash

    Why not use a bit of flash to change up the atmosphere in your shots? To turn it on, use the switch on the front of the camera (effective flash range is 3m).

  • Accessorize your way!

    The strap hole allows you to attach your own unique strap, so why not add a neck, shoulder or wrist strap to your camera that fits your style (strap not included).
