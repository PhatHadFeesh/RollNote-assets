Olympus Standard above right

Olympus Standard, 127 SLR 1938, Extremely Rare, £?????

Olympus made 10 examples of this Leica style Rangefinder interchangeable lens camera. Only one of these cameras ever escaped. It's highly unlikely anyone will find it; hence the valuation being five figures. If you did, what might you pay for the one of the world's rarest cameras? Actually my wife has seen Standard number 102, the one here - her comments? "It's huge, it's hideous, you wouldn't want it on your shelf!" So there you have it. Notice the red viewfinder.

Olympus 35 Model 2 with Takatiho Tokyo Zuiko Lens Extremely Rare £400

About 250 of this type/configuration were made before the company adopted the Olympus Zuiko brand in 1949. This example is the 48th commercial CatsPaw ever made.
